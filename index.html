About Us

Nics Tea Shop freshly brews and mixes tea leaves with milk, flavors, and toppings like tapioca pearls and jelly to craft milk teas, fruit teas, and specialty drinks. We deliver these crowd-pleasing refreshments to students, offices, homes, and events. Because almost everyone loves milk tea, a well-run shop can generate steady profits while bringing joy to customers.
